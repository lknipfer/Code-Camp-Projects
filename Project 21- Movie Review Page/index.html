Movie Review: Rise Beyond 2

Rise Beyond 2 is a short film following a determined young athlete navigating a career-threatening injury. The film focuses on his journey to reclaim his dreams by overcoming intense physical pain, mental hurdles, self-doubt, and immense pressure. It is an inspirational story highlighting resilience, perseverance, and mental strength to push beyond limits.
