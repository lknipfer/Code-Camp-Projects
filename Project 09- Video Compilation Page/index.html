Dog Training Experience

When we got our dog from the shelter almost a year ago, we had no idea what we were in for. We started watching countless dog training videos on YouTube and Blue has now gone through 2 dog trainers in the past year. While he is still a work in progress, I can say that we have learned a lot through this experience.

Tom Davis Videos

Tom Davis had become one of our favorite person to watch when it came to learning how to teach a reactive dog to not be reactive around other dogs. His videos were helpful and relatable. We bought a better leash and treat pouch through his website.

Beckman Videos

Beckman had been another guy that we liked to watch on YouTube when it came to learning door manners and leash reactivity.

Off Leash K9 Training

Back to where it all started. This was where Blue learned basic commands and we learned how to use the ecollar with him. Even though we aren't using the ecollar anymore and have switched him to the prong collar, we can't forget where it all started.
